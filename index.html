Projects

A corpus of ~290 hours of individual and small-group math and computer science tutoring session transcripts with middle and high-school students.

Transcripts are being de-identified, cleaned, and standardized into a turn-level schema (JSONL), segmented into turns, exchanges, and problem episodes, and annotated.

Tutor move annotations (focus, probing, telling, generic) are adapted from MathDial (Macina et al., 2023) and student engagement (passive, active, constructive, interactive) uses the ICAP framework (Chi & Wylie, 2014).

From August 2025 to November 2026, ~200 hours of computer science and ~90 hours of mathematics tutoring have been recorded.

All sessions are recorded with student and guardian consent. Data kept private due to identification risk.

Built a Python pipeline using OpenCV that turns pen-and-paper reflections into structured data for teachers to gain long-term engagement metrics.

Tracking engagement of ~400 high school English learners in a rural Japanese high school using worksheet-reader data.

Findings are being shared with JET Program supervisors to improve teaching methods and provide insights into the classroom.

Data is being collected and analyzed with the full permission of teachers and school administrators. Only anonymized statistics and plots will be shared publicly.
